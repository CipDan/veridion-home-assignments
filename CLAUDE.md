## Guidelines

Read at the beginning of every session the corresponding CLAUDE.md file, inferred from the prompt's description. If no clear CLAUDE.md file can be identified from the prompt, ask the prompter for it specifically before proceeding with anything else.

When working on a specific project, both the repository-root CLAUDE.md and the project-specific CLAUDE.md apply. The project-specific CLAUDE.md instructions take precedence over any conflicting instructions in the root CLAUDE.md. Root instructions remain applicable unless explicitly overridden by the project-specific file.
